# Welcome

This is a collection of my security writeups, CTF solutions, and technical notes — documenting problems I've worked through, including the dead ends along the way, not just the final solution.

## Browse by category

- CTFs contains my writeups for CTFs.
- Forage contains my writeups for virtual job simulation experiences taken on the platform.
- Over The Wire contains my writeups for the war games provided by the OverTheWire community.

## About this site

Notes are written in [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) and published here using [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/). Not everything in my working notes is published — what you see here has been deliberately chosen and cleaned up for sharing.

You can also use the search (top left) or explorer sidebar to browse everything directly, or check the [tag index](https://claude.ai/tags) to browse by topic.
